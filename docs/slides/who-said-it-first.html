The problemOur model of reality is built from retold text. Quotes get trimmed, reworded and credited to the wrong person, and AI models learn the drifted version.

What we builtA tracer over four months of news: about 4 million articles, January to April 2020. Give it “X said that…”; it finds the quotes that say it and the earliest article that carried them.

How it improvesA stronger AI model studies the tracer's mistakes and suggests small fixes. Three fixes passed, and on questions it never studied, correct first reports rose from 60% to 86%.

How a quote drifts
SaidA

Someone says the words, once, at one moment.

ReportedA

The first article carries them, with a time stamp.

TrimmedA

Other outlets cut the quote to fit.

RewordedA

Posts and summaries paraphrase it.

ReattributedB

A better-known name gets the credit, and AI models learn that version.

How it works · 2Tracing a statement

Three steps take a statement to its first report

1Shortlist

Find 30 candidates

Given a sentence like “X said that…”, pull the 30 quotes that share the most distinctive words with it.

Best clue: the speaker's name.
Only quotes that existed before the statement was published.

2Careful reading

Keep the ones that say it

A small, fast AI model reads the shortlist and picks the quotes that actually say what the statement claims.

The same claim, not just the same topic.

3First report

Pick the earliest article

For the quotes it picked, list every article that carried them and choose the earliest one.

Only articles with a precise publication time.

Only the reading step uses an AI model. The shortlist is a word search, the first report a sort by time.

ImplicationsBeyond the news

News is the simple case; next we can trace where ideas and inventions come from

From words to ideas
Reconceptualizationsthe same thing, seen anew
Inventionsthe same mechanism
Ideasthe same idea, new context
Claimsthe same meaning
Quotesthe same words · today

Each step up needs a looser sense of “the same”. The method stays: find the versions, order them in time, check the earliest.

Only in the open

We can only trace what is published openly. Then credit goes to whoever shared an idea first, and that rewards contributing to the commons.
